·The platform

Every Biomechanix app runs on the same on-device engine. A physiotherapist, coach or trainer teaches it what correct movement looks like; it does the watching in between — at home, in the gym, wherever the expert isn't standing next to you.

It doesn't just detect that you moved. It understands the movement: the joint that drives it, the joints that should stay still, and what a small compensation usually means.

01 / See

See

The camera finds every joint in your body in real time, on your phone — catching what's too fast or too subtle for the eye.

02 / Measure

Measure

Those joints become angles, range of motion, left–right symmetry and hold times.

03 / Understand

Understand

Each movement is modelled as its driving joint, the still joints, and the compensations to watch for.

04 / Respond

Respond

Reps are counted and form is scored. A spoken cue arrives in time to fix the next rep.

Your privacy, by design. Everything the camera sees is processed on your phone — no images or video ever leave your device.

Before you ask

·FAQ
Do I need a wearable or extra hardware? +
No. A phone and about 2 × 2 m of floor space is enough for every workout, exam or assessment.
Is a Sameeksha report a diagnosis? +
No. It reports observations as possibilities for a qualified professional to confirm. If symptoms are severe or worsening, seek medical care directly.
Will Physio replace my physiotherapist's judgement? +
No. It measures and delivers the prescription at the dose the clinician sets. Exercise selection, dosage and interpretation stay with them.
Do I need an account? +
Not to get started. Fitness has a guest mode, Physio works offline, and Sameeksha can back up your results if you sign in.
Which languages does Sameeksha support? +
English, Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada — including the safety-screen wording.
