The MoveMentor platform

Every Biomechanix app runs on the same on-device engine. It doesn't just detect that you moved. It understands the movement: the joint that drives it, the joints that should stay still, and what a small compensation usually means.

Step 1

See

The camera finds every joint in your body in real time, on your phone — catching what's too fast or too subtle for the eye.

Your privacy, by design. Everything the camera sees is processed on your phone — no images or video ever leave your device. Only results like reps and scores sync, and only if you choose to sign in.

What “markerless” actually means

No markers, no sensors, nothing to charge or misplace. “Markerless motion capture” just means the camera itself finds your joints as you move, using a model trained to recognise a human body in ordinary video — the same underlying idea used in sports-science labs, running on the phone already in your hand.

Built to extend the expert, not replace them

A physiotherapist, coach or trainer teaches the engine what correct movement looks like. It does the watching in between — at home, in the gym, wherever the expert isn't standing next to you.

The care loop

Clinic to living room, and back.

1 · Examine

Guided tests measure peak range, symmetry and hold time.

2 · Prescribe

Set reps, holds and range targets. Contraindications are checked.

3 · Deliver

Send as a QR code, a file, or online to a linked account.

4 · Perform

The patient trains at home in the MoveMentor Fitness app, with real-time feedback at the prescribed dose.

5 · Review

Completed sets, reps and peak range come back for each prescription.

How the assessment works

Five guided steps, one joint at a time.

Step 1
About you

Age, sex and side, so results compare against people like you.

Step 2
Your symptoms

A questionnaire for the joint you chose, kept in your own words.

Step 3
Safety screen

Warning-sign questions that take priority over everything else.

Step 4
Movement tests

Short camera-guided tests measure range, control and symmetry, including supine straight-leg checks.

Step 5
Walking check

For knee and back, a short recording of how you walk.

Your report — more than a single score

01
Individual findings

Each observation is graded normal, mild, moderate, significant or a safety indicator. The same issue across several tests counts for more.

02
Three risk scores

Symptoms, movement and safety, each scored out of 100 and weighted for the joint you assessed. Skipping a test never counts as failing it.

03
One clear next step

A warning sign always overrides the scores and points to medical care. Otherwise, a practical plan.
