The MoveMentor platform

Every Biomechanix app runs on the same on-device engine. It doesn't just detect that you moved — it understands the movement: which joint drives it, which joints should stay still, and what a small compensation usually means.

Step 1

See

Your phone’s camera tracks every joint as you move, faster and more precisely than the human eye can follow — on your phone, not in a data centre.

Your privacy, built in. Everything the camera sees is analysed on your phone. Images and video never leave your device. Only results like reps and scores sync, and only if you choose to sign in.

What “markerless” actually means

No markers, no sensors, nothing to charge or misplace. “Markerless motion capture” just means the camera itself finds your joints as you move, using a model trained to recognise a human body in ordinary video — the same underlying idea used in sports-science labs, running on the phone already in your hand.

Built to extend the expert, not replace them

A physiotherapist, coach or trainer teaches the model what correct movement looks like for their specialty. They set the targets and make the calls that need a professional. MoveMentor does the watching in between — at home, in the gym, wherever the expert isn't standing next to you.

The care loop

One loop, from the clinic to the living room and back.

1 · Examine

Guided tests measure peak range, symmetry and hold time.

2 · Prescribe

Choose exercises, set reps, holds and range-of-motion targets.

3 · Deliver

Send as a QR code, a file, or online to a linked account.

4 · Perform

Patient exercises at home with real-time feedback at the prescribed dose.

5 · Review

Completed sets, reps and peak range come back for each prescription.

For clinicians — objective measures, without a goniometer in your hand

01Physio

Camera-measured exams

Guided tests record achieved range, symmetry and hold duration, saved to the patient's record.

02Physio

Dose-adjusted prescriptions

03Physio

Contraindication checks

Prescriptions are checked against the patient's condition. Absolute contraindications block the exercise; relative ones show a warning you review.

05Physio

06Physio

Works offline

For patients

  • Exact reps, holds and targets from your prescription
  • Real-time cues when your form drifts
  • A record of every session against what was prescribed

Will this replace my judgement?

No. MoveMentor measures and delivers; you decide. Exercise selection, dosage and interpretation of measurements stay the clinician's professional call — contraindication checks flag what needs your review, they don't override it.

A tool for clinicians, not a replacement. MoveMentor Physio supports a qualified physiotherapist's assessment and treatment. Live analysis stays on the phone, and patient records created in the clinic are stored on the clinician's device.

Sameeksha · Musculoskeletal self-assessment

Understand your joint pain, and know what to do next.

Sameeksha takes you through a structured check of your knee, lower back, shoulder or elbow using your phone and its camera. At the end you get a clear screening report and one recommended next step.

Screening, not diagnosis. Sameeksha reports what it observes and what may be contributing to it, always as possibilities for a qualified professional to confirm. It doesn't diagnose conditions or replace an examination. If symptoms are severe or worsening, seek medical care.

A movement check-up on your phone.

About 10 minutes. No wearables, no lab.

Designed to be touchless, so you can answer from across the room without walking back to the phone.

  • Point to where it hurts, and the camera confirms the spot.
  • Answer by raising a hand on the side of your answer, saying it out loud, or tapping it.

How the assessment works

Five guided steps, one joint at a time.

Step 1
About you

Age, sex and side, so results compare against people like you.

Step 2
Your symptoms

A questionnaire for the joint you chose, kept in your own words.

Step 3
Safety screen

Warning-sign questions that take priority over everything else.

Step 4
Movement tests

Short camera-guided tests measure range, control and symmetry.

Step 5
Walking check

For knee and back, a short recording of how you walk.

Your report — more than a single score

01
Individual findings

Each observation is graded normal, mild, moderate, significant or a safety indicator. The same issue across several tests counts for more.

02
Three risk dimensions

Symptoms, movement and safety are each scored out of 100, weighted for the joint you assessed. Skipping a test never counts as failing it.

03
One clear next step

A warning sign always overrides the scores and points to medical care. Otherwise, a practical plan, with home exercises where appropriate.
